v1.1.1 — Editor fixes from on-device testing (Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 6):

- Export coordinate fix: placed text and signatures now flatten at exactly
  the position shown in the editor. Earlier the exported text drifted upward
  (signatures slightly, text considerably) because the on-screen Compose
  preview included font padding the exporter did not. Editor and exporter
  now share the same glyph-top baseline.
- Resize handles moved fully OUTSIDE the selection box, so the blue handles
  no longer cover the content underneath when placing an X in a small
  checkbox or sizing a tight box. Hit area expanded to match.
- Undo / Redo (20-step) for move, resize, add, delete, and text edits.
  A drag is one undo step, not one per pixel.
- Bottom action menu redesigned as a 2-column vertical-scroll grid showing
  two rows (four buttons) at a time. Undo and Redo are pinned to the top
  row so they are always visible without scrolling.

(All features from v1.1.0 retained. Local/offline, no ads/cloud/login.)
